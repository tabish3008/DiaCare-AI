Ayurveda: a traditional system


Ayurveda is a traditional system of medicine that originated in India and is practised widely today. Per NCCIH, evidence for Ayurvedic treatments for diabetes is limited, and some preparations have been found to contain harmful metals such as lead, mercury or arsenic, or to interact with medicines. This site does not recommend any herb, kadha, supplement or product, and never suggests one based on a glucose reading. Do not start, stop, or change prescribed medicine on your own.


Questions to take to your clinician
  • Could this product interact with my prescribed medicines?
  • Is this product independently tested for contaminants?
  • Could it affect my blood glucose or the way I monitor it?
  • Is it safe alongside my other conditions or if I am pregnant?
