About DiaCare AI

DiaCare AI is an educational tool for organizing patient-provided health records and creating a report for discussion with a qualified healthcare professional. It does not diagnose conditions, predict outcomes, or replace clinical care.

Ayurveda

Ayurveda is a traditional system of medicine originating in India. Evidence for Ayurvedic treatments for diabetes is limited, and some products may contain harmful contaminants or interact with prescribed medicines. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional before using any supplement or traditional preparation. Never stop or change prescribed medicine based on this site.

Learn more from NCCIH ↗

Yoga and wellbeing

For some people, yoga can support flexibility, balance, relaxation, and general wellbeing. Research on yoga for diabetes is still limited, and the right activity depends on each person’s health. Ask your healthcare professional what is safe for you.
